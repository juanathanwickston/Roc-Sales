Frequently Asked Questions

ROC Academy is Payroc's centralized sales training platform. It pulls together curated content from multiple LMS systems, interactive games, AI coaching, leaderboards, and progress tracking, all in one place. Think of it as your single hub for everything you need to ramp up and stay sharp.
ROC Academy is designed for Payroc sales representatives, from brand new hires during their first 30 days to seasoned reps looking to sharpen specific skills. Managers also benefit from visibility into team readiness and leaderboard performance.
Content is organized into phase-based modules covering product knowledge, the sales process, objection handling, territory management, compensation, and coaching. Each module contains curated videos, documents, and interactive activities. Some are sourced from existing Payroc LMS platforms, others are built natively for ROC Academy.
Each game targets a specific skill area. Feature Factory is a Tetris-style sorting game that tests product knowledge. Objection Blitz is a rapid-fire quiz on handling objections. Sales Floor walks you through realistic customer scenarios. Games are scored, tracked, and contribute to your Readiness Index and leaderboard ranking.
Yes. Managers with admin access can view the leaderboard, export progress reports, and see individual Readiness Index scores. This visibility helps managers identify who needs support and recognize top performers.
The Readiness Index measures competency across six skill areas: Product Knowledge, Sales Process, Objection Handling, Territory Management, Comp & Margin IQ, and Coaching. Each area is weighted based on its correlation with revenue performance. Your scores from games, quizzes, and activities feed into each skill area to produce an overall readiness percentage.
Yes. ROC Academy is built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it directly to your phone's home screen and use it like a native app. It works on any device with a modern browser. No app store download required.
ROC Certification is a structured program that requires completing all core modules and achieving minimum scores across skill areas. Once certified, your status is visible on the leaderboard and your profile. It demonstrates that you've mastered the fundamentals needed to succeed in the field.
