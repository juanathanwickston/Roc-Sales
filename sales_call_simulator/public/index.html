Call Guide

Module 1

Turn this suspect into a qualified lead. You're calling a restaurant owner referred by a friend — he's not expecting your call and knows nothing about Payroc.

Remember: A suspect fits your Ideal Customer Profile but has shown no genuine interest. Your job is to earn engagement through discovery, not push a product.

Customer-focused reps seek to understand needs before presenting solutions. Show genuine curiosity about their business.

  • Ask about their restaurant, how long they've been in business
  • Reference the referral naturally (Dave)
  • Show you're interested in THEM, not just the sale
"Dave mentioned you run a great place — how long have you been in the restaurant business?"

Use open-ended questions to uncover challenges. You can't solve a problem you don't understand.

  • Ask about their current payment processing setup
  • Explore frustrations (fees, speed, reporting)
  • Ask "What happens if nothing changes?"
  • Quantify the pain — how much is it costing them?
"What are the biggest headaches with your current payment setup?"

BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline
CHAMP: Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization

  • Budget: "What budget has been allocated for this?"
  • Authority: "Who else is involved in this decision?"
  • Need: "What critical pain points are you addressing?"
  • Timeline: "When do you need a solution in place?"
"Besides yourself, who would need to be part of the decision to switch processors?"

Propose a clear, low-commitment next step. Do NOT push for a sale on the first call.

  • Suggest a demo or follow-up with decision-makers present
  • Set clear expectations for the follow-up
  • Confirm their interest and timeline
"Would it make sense to set up a quick 20-minute demo with you and Maria so she can see how it works?"
  • Feature-dumping before asking about their situation
  • Treating a suspect like a prospect from minute one
  • Not asking about other decision-makers (authority)
  • Failing to quantify pain points
  • Pushing for a close instead of a follow-up
