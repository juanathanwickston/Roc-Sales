# Sales Rep Briefing: Pastor David Miller (New Hope Community Church)
## Course Integration: LMS Modules 1–3 to Simulator Handoff

This briefing document aligns the ROC Sales Process stages with the merchant profile of Pastor David Miller. It serves as the foundation for the content sales reps will study in LMS Modules 1–3 before taking the live simulation in Stage 4.

---

## Stage 1 – Identifying the Customer (LMS Module 1)

### 1. The Sales Funnel & ICP Alignment
*   **Target Segment:** Places of worship, churches, local charities, and community nonprofits.
*   **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Match:** New Hope Community Church is an established church in Western Pennsylvania with approximately 350 active members. The church operates youth programs and volunteer services, relying heavily on donations.
*   **Funnel Status:**
    *   *Suspect:* Fits the profile, but has not shown interest.
    *   *Lead:* David clicked a link in a newsletter about "Engaging Younger Givers in Local Congregations."
    *   *Prospect (Qualified):* The rep must verify David's goals and fundraising constraints during the call.

### 2. CHAMP Qualification Framework
Before the call, the sales rep should project how CHAMP will apply to David:
*   **Challenges:** Inconsistent donations, manual tracking of checks/cash, volunteer burnout due to administrative tasks, and difficulty engaging younger donors.
*   **Authority:** Pastor David Miller is the spiritual and community leader. He makes major operational decisions in consultation with a church board, but has strong personal influence.
*   **Money:** The church is raising funds for a specific project: repairing the community basketball court for their youth basketball program. Funds are tight.
*   **Prioritization:** High priority because the court repair project is important to the community, and fundraising momentum needs to be sustained.

### 3. Product Fit: Roc Giving
The correct product for this merchant is **Roc Giving**. It addresses:
*   **Recurring Donations:** Introduces digital, recurring tithing options for consistent monthly cash flow.
*   **Text-to-Give & Mobile Campaigns:** Simplifies fundraising for specific projects (like the basketball court) on-site or during services.
*   **Donor Management:** Automates contribution tracking and tax receipts, reducing volunteer administrative hours.

## Stage 2 – Ask for the Appointment (LMS Module 2)

### 1. First Impressions & Behavioral Style Adaptation
*   **Merchant Behavioral Style:** Talker-Supporter Hybrid (warm, conversational, relational, deeply mission-focused).
*   **Rep Communication Strategy:**
    *   *Do:* Build rapport naturally, share stories, connect your service to their community mission, use partnership-oriented language, and demonstrate empathy.
    *   *Avoid:* Sounds transactional or pushy, using heavy technological buzzwords, or focusing entirely on the checkout terminal/fees.
*   **Elevator Pitch:**
    *   *Problem:* Many local ministries struggle with inconsistent tithes and manually tracking donations, which takes time away from their community work.
    *   *Solution:* We help faith-based organizations make giving simpler and more accessible.
    *   *Unique Value:* A platform that encourages recurring giving and supports community fundraisers (like youth sports projects) without feeling transactional.

### 2. Acknowledging the Interruption & Setting the Call
*   **The Premise:** During prospecting, the rep reached out, asked about their community programs, and scheduled a warm, relaxed discussion to learn about the church's basketball court campaign.
*   **The Handoff:** The rep must open the call with a friendly, warm introduction, asking how the community project is going.

---

## Stage 3 – Prep for the Appointment (LMS Module 3)

### 1. Designing Need Development Questions
The rep must prepare specific questions using the **GAP Model** to guide David from his current state to a desired state.

| GAP Element | Rep Strategy / Question Design | Target Information to Uncover |
|---|---|---|
| **Current Situation** | *"How do members currently support the church and the basketball court project?"* | Traditional checks, cash envelopes in collection plate. |
| **Risks / Consequences** | *"What happens to your fundraising when members miss a service or travel?"* | Inconsistent donations, lack of tracking, momentum stalls. |
| **Desired Situation** | *"If you could provide members with a simple way to set up recurring tithes online or tithe via mobile, how would that help?"* | More consistent giving, younger donor engagement, clear campaign tracking. |
| **Benefits / Rewards** | *"What would automating those tax statements and deposit reports mean for your volunteers?"* | Reduces volunteer burnout, allows them to focus on ministry. |

---

## Stage 4 – Make the Sale (Simulator Call 1)

### 1. Customer-Focused Discovery
*   **Goal:** Focus on the church's mission and community impact. If you pitch too early or sound transactional, David will object: *"We already have ways to collect donations"* or *"We don't want giving to feel like a transaction."*
*   **Active Listening:** Let David share stories about the AAU youth program and the community center.
*   **Uncovering Hidden Pain:** Uncover check/cash manual limits -> then volunteer workload -> and finally, the concern that campaign momentum will stall and younger givers will disconnect.
*   **Next Step Close:** Ask for a follow-up discussion to show him a simple campaign page mockup for their basketball court fundraiser.

---

## Stage 5 – Close the Sale (Simulator Call 2)

### 1. Linking Solution to Needs
*   Reference the specific goals discussed in Call 1 (the basketball court project, volunteer burnout, and keeping giving personal).
*   Present **Roc Giving** as a tool that supports the church's outreach.
*   Emphasize simplicity and text-to-give features for the court project.

### 2. Handling Objections and Closing
*   Anticipate objections: *"Most people here still prefer checks"* or *"We're not very tech-savvy."*
*   Demonstrate how simple it is for older members to use, and secure a trust-based commitment.
