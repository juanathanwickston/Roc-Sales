# Sales Rep Briefing: Sam Patel (QuickStop Market)
## Course Integration: LMS Modules 1–3 to Simulator Handoff

This briefing document aligns the ROC Sales Process stages with the merchant profile of Sam Patel. It serves as the foundation for the content sales reps will study in LMS Modules 1–3 before taking the live simulation in Stage 4.

---

## Stage 1 – Identifying the Customer (LMS Module 1)

### 1. The Sales Funnel & ICP Alignment
*   **Target Segment:** Independent convenience stores / small retail.
*   **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Match:** QuickStop Market is a busy neighborhood convenience store in suburban Michigan. It operates on thin margins, has high transaction volumes, and manages inventory/pricing manually.
*   **Funnel Status:** 
    *   *Suspect:* Fits the profile, but has not shown interest.
    *   *Lead:* Sam responded to a prospecting email about reducing manual pricing errors.
    *   *Prospect (Qualified):* The rep must verify Sam's challenges and decision authority during the call.

### 2. CHAMP Qualification Framework
Before the call, the sales rep should project how CHAMP will apply to Sam:
*   **Challenges:** High credit card processing fees, inventory leakage, manual pricing labor, and compliance risks (EBT/age verification warnings).
*   **Authority:** Sam Patel is the sole Owner/Operator. He is the final decision-maker but is highly protective of his time.
*   **Money:** Margins are tight. Any solution must demonstrate immediate ROI or cost-neutral transition.
*   **Prioritization:** Sam works long hours. Solving operational inefficiencies is a priority *only* if it saves him time immediately.

### 3. Product Fit: Bodega AI
The correct product for this merchant is **Bodega AI**. It addresses:
*   **Margin Leakage:** Automates scan data and pricing.
*   **Inventory Control:** Replaces spreadsheets and manual counts with automated tracking.
*   **Compliance Risk:** Reduces errors in age verification and EBT transactions.

## Stage 2 – Ask for the Appointment (LMS Module 2)

### 1. First Impressions & Behavioral Style Adaptation
*   **Merchant Behavioral Style:** Controller (analytical, efficient, highly skeptical).
*   **Rep Communication Strategy:** 
    *   *Do:* Get to the point quickly, use data/specific metrics, respect his time, and be direct.
    *   *Avoid:* Rapport-building small talk, vague generalities, or long product introductions.
*   **Elevator Pitch:** 
    *   *Problem:* Convenience stores lose 2–3% in margin due to manual pricing errors and missed rebates.
    *   *Solution:* We help independent markets automate their pricing and capture tobacco rebates.
    *   *Unique Value:* A system tailored for convenience retail that saves 5+ hours a week.

### 2. Acknowledging the Interruption & Setting the Call
*   **The Premise:** During prospecting, the rep called Sam, acknowledged that he was busy running the store, and asked for a brief 10-minute window to talk. Sam reluctantly agreed to a call on Wednesday before his evening rush.
*   **The Handoff:** The rep must open the call knowing that Sam is expecting them, but has very low patience and is waiting to see if this is a waste of time.

---

## Stage 3 – Prep for the Appointment (LMS Module 3)

### 1. Designing Need Development Questions
The rep must prepare specific questions using the **GAP Model** to guide Sam from his current state to a desired state without triggering objections.

| GAP Element | Rep Strategy / Question Design | Target Information to Uncover |
|---|---|---|
| **Current Situation** | *"How are you currently tracking inventory and updating shelf prices?"* | Manual spreadsheets, staff walking the aisles, loose counts. |
| **Risks / Consequences** | *"What happens when pricing updates are delayed or staff make errors?"* | Lost margin, stockouts, pricing inconsistencies. |
| **Desired Situation** | *"If you could automate scan data and pricing, what would that look like for your day-to-day?"* | Time savings, automated tobacco rebates, margin protection. |
| **Benefits / Rewards** | *"What would saving 5+ hours of manual work after hours mean for your store operations?"* | Less overtime, fewer EBT/age verification compliance worries. |

---

## Stage 4 – Make the Sale (Simulator Call 1)

### 1. Customer-Focused Discovery
*   **Goal:** Do NOT pitch Bodega AI immediately. Lead with discovery. If you pitch early, Sam will object: *"I already have a POS system."*
*   **Active Listening:** Let Sam speak. Adapt to his short, direct answers.
*   **Uncovering Hidden Pain:** Guide Sam to talk about manual pricing errors -> then missed tobacco rebates -> and finally, EBT/age verification compliance warnings.
*   **Next Step Close:** Once value is established, ask for a scheduled, deeper follow-up call to review a tailored pricing proposal.

---

## Stage 5 – Close the Sale (Simulator Call 2)

### 1. Linking Solution to Needs
*   Reference the specific issues uncovered in Call 1 (e.g., manual shelf checks, EBT audit fears, missed tobacco rebates).
*   Present **Bodega AI** as the direct answer to those challenges.
*   Use the formula: *"Because you mentioned [Pain], what we do is [Feature], and what that means for you is [Benefit]."*

### 2. Handling Price and Closing
*   Anticipate Sam's analytical nature. Provide clear cost vs. value calculations.
*   Secure the buying commitment.
