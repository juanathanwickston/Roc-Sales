# Sales Rep Briefing: Mike Turner (Precision Plumbing & Drain)
## Course Integration: LMS Modules 1–3 to Simulator Handoff

This briefing document aligns the ROC Sales Process stages with the merchant profile of Mike Turner. It serves as the foundation for the content sales reps will study in LMS Modules 1–3 before taking the live simulation in Stage 4.

---

## Stage 1 – Identifying the Customer (LMS Module 1)

### 1. The Sales Funnel & ICP Alignment
*   **Target Segment:** Home services, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and contracting businesses.
*   **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Match:** Precision Plumbing & Drain is a residential plumbing company in Columbus, Ohio. It operates 4 trucks with 4 field technicians. Business is busy, but back-office operations are manual, leading to billing bottlenecks.
*   **Funnel Status:**
    *   *Suspect:* Fits the profile, but has not shown interest.
    *   *Lead:* Mike responded to a marketing ad targeting "Reducing Time to Invoice for Field Contractors."
    *   *Prospect (Qualified):* The rep must verify Mike's challenges and decision authority during the call.

### 2. CHAMP Qualification Framework
Before the call, the sales rep should project how CHAMP will apply to Mike:
*   **Challenges:** Delayed payment collection (payments take 2–3 weeks), technicians collecting payments inconsistently in the field, manual invoicing backlogs, and seasonal cash flow stress.
*   **Authority:** Mike Turner is the sole Owner/Operator. He is highly direct and handles both field work and major business decisions.
*   **Money:** Cash flow is the primary concern. Value is defined by speed-to-pay and reducing administrative overhead.
*   **Prioritization:** High priority because administrative billing delays are directly constricting cash flow and technician productivity.

### 3. Product Fit: Roc Services
The correct product for this merchant is **Roc Services**. It addresses:
*   **In-Field Payment Processing:** Allows field techs to invoice and collect card/ACH payments on-site immediately upon job completion.
*   **Dispatch & Scheduling Visibility:** Unifies calendar, job tracking, and invoicing in a single field-management tool.
*   **QuickBooks Sync:** Eliminates double-entry by syncing invoices and payments directly to accounting.

## Stage 2 – Ask for the Appointment (LMS Module 2)

### 1. First Impressions & Behavioral Style Adaptation
*   **Merchant Behavioral Style:** Doer (fast-paced, direct, results-oriented, highly impatient).
*   **Rep Communication Strategy:**
    *   *Do:* Be blunt and concise, focus on cash flow and payment speed, get to the point within the first 30 seconds, and use concrete operational examples.
    *   *Avoid:* Long introductory statements, corporate buzzwords, small talk, or theoretical explanations.
*   **Elevator Pitch:**
    *   *Problem:* Contractors lose hours chasing unpaid invoices and wait weeks for checks to clear.
    *   *Solution:* We help field service businesses schedule, invoice, and collect payments on-site.
    *   *Unique Value:* Cut billing delays from 2 weeks to 2 minutes, getting your crew paid before they leave the job.

### 2. Acknowledging the Interruption & Setting the Call
*   **The Premise:** During prospecting, the rep reached out, immediately acknowledged that Mike was busy on a job site, and asked for a tight, 5-minute conversation. Mike agreed to take a quick call during a break.
*   **The Handoff:** The rep must open the call with high energy and zero fluff, acknowledging Mike's direct nature.

---

## Stage 3 – Prep for the Appointment (LMS Module 3)

### 1. Designing Need Development Questions
The rep must prepare specific questions using the **GAP Model** to guide Mike from his current state to a desired state.

| GAP Element | Rep Strategy / Question Design | Target Information to Uncover |
|---|---|---|
| **Current Situation** | *"How are your techs currently writing up jobs and taking payments in the field?"* | Paper invoices, checks collected in field, office typing up invoices later. |
| **Risks / Consequences** | *"What is the average turnaround time between completing a plumbing job and getting paid?"* | Invoices take days to send, payments take 2–3 weeks to arrive. |
| **Desired Situation** | *"If your techs could generate invoices and take credit cards on the spot, how would that impact your billing?"* | Instant payments, zero office double-entry, improved cash flow. |
| **Benefits / Rewards** | *"What would eliminating that manual billing backlog mean for your office workload?"* | Saves hours of admin chasing, reduces slow-season cash flow stress. |

---

## Stage 4 – Make the Sale (Simulator Call 1)

### 1. Customer-Focused Discovery
*   **Goal:** Focus on payment speed and office efficiency. If you pitch too early or move too slowly, Mike will object: *"We've always done it this way"* or *"Can you just show me what this does?"*
*   **Active Listening:** Handle his interruptions confidently. Do not argue.
*   **Uncovering Hidden Pain:** Uncover inconsistent in-field payments -> then payment delays -> and finally, seasonal cash flow anxiety and lost estimates due to slow follow-up.
*   **Next Step Close:** Ask for a brief 10-minute online walkthrough to show the software interface, emphasizing that he can see exactly how the technicians use it on a phone.

---

## Stage 5 – Close the Sale (Simulator Call 2)

### 1. Linking Solution to Needs
*   Reference his specific challenges (e.g., techs losing paperwork, office chasing checks, 2-week delays).
*   Demonstrate how **Roc Services** automates mobile invoicing.
*   Clearly illustrate the cash flow improvement (getting paid on-site).

### 2. Handling Objections and Closing
*   Anticipate his objections: *"My guys won't use it"* or *"I already use QuickBooks."*
*   Demonstrate simple technician workflows and seamless QuickBooks sync, then ask directly for a decision.
