4. Critical Guidelines
Product Focus: Pitch the designated software solution ([Product Name]). Pitching standard credit card terminals instead of the designated product is a failure. (Saying "Payroc" is acceptable, but you must focus on the correct product).
Reference Prior Connection: Establish continuity by referencing the previous scheduled appointment. Do not open the call as a cold call.
Avoid Technical Jargon: Use simple, direct language. Do not reference internal sales framework terminology.
Objection Validation: Validate customer objections before responding. Do not argue or become defensive.
