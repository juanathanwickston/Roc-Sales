# Module 1 Curriculum: Identifying the Customer

This module focuses on core selling fundamentals and the initial stages of the sales funnel.

## 1. Fundamentals
### 1.1 Selling Mindset & Belief Alignment
Aligned sales performance is driven by mindset across five dimensions:
- **View of Selling**: Sales creates real value for customers.
- **View of Abilities**: Confidence in one's skills and capability to succeed.
- **Values**: Integrity and honesty as guiding principles.
- **Commitment to Activities**: Consistency in executing the required sales activities.
- **Belief in Product**: Conviction that the solutions create meaningful value.

### 1.2 Customer-Focused vs Product-Focused
- **Customer-Focused Sales Rep**: Seeks to understand needs and motives *before* presenting solutions.
- **Product-Focused Sales Rep**: Leads with features and benefits without identifying true needs.
- **Goal**: Transition from "Presenting Products" to "Solving Problems".

## 2. The Sales Funnel
- **Suspect**: Fits ICP but has zero proven interest. Focus: Qualification.
- **Lead**: A suspect who has taken action (downloaded content, responded to outreach). Focus: Evaluation.
- **Prospect**: A qualified lead with defined need, budget, authority, and timeline. Focus: Relationship building and closing.

## 3. Qualification Frameworks
Reorganized into practical coaching steps for the simulator.

### BANT
- **Budget**: Financial alignment and priority.
- **Authority**: Decision-makers involved.
- **Need**: Critical pain points and measure of success.
- **Timeline**: Urgency and internal deadlines.

### CHAMP (Alternative)
- **Challenges**: Leading with the problem/impact.
- **Authority**: Stakeholders and process.
- **Money**: Financial impact and ROI.
- **Prioritization**: Why is this a priority *now*?

## 4. Successful Outcome
The goal of this module is NOT the sale. It is:
1. Identifying decision-makers (Authority).
2. Confirming budget/ROI alignment (Money/Budget).
3. Understanding the decision process and timeline.
4. **Earning the right to ask for an appointment (The Next Stage).**
