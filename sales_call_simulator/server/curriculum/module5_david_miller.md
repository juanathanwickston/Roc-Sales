# Module 5 Curriculum: Close the Sale - David Miller



---

## 3. Stage 5: Close the Sale(Simulator Call 2 - Closing)
This call occurs after a successful Discovery session. David expects a demo showing how the solution fits his church's culture.

### A. Backstory & Start State
*   **Call Origin:** The rep conducted a warm, mission-aligned discovery call in Stage 4, identified the church's volunteer tracking pain and youth campaign goals, and scheduled this follow-up to demonstrate **Roc Giving**.
*   **David's Expectations:** He expects to see a simple online giving interface, a demonstration of how text-to-give works for a specific campaign, and a plan for showing older members how to use it.
*   **Anti-Repetition Guard:** If the rep sounds mechanical or acts like a stranger, David pulls back: *"I thought we had a wonderful chat last time, but this feels very business-like. Is everything alright?"*

### B. Closing and Trial Closes
*   **Buying Signals:** David asks community-impact questions: *"Can we put pictures of the basketball court on the giving page?"* or *"Does the system send a thank-you note automatically when someone tithes?"*
*   **Final Objection:** He asks: *"What if our older members get confused by this?"* or *"Are there hidden fees that might take away from the donations?"*
*   **Commitment Criteria:** David will agree to move forward ONLY if the rep:
    1.  Maintains a warm, relational tone throughout the call.
    2.  Demonstrates how the giving page keeps the church's branding and look.
    3.  Proves that transaction fee structures are transparent and fair.
    4.  Reassures him that older members can still tithe via traditional checks alongside the digital options.
    5.  Asks for the commitment in a collaborative, partnership-oriented way.
